### Root Cause
Previously, `Contains.as_set()` raised `NotImplementedError()` directly on `Contains`, preventing boolean expressions containing `Contains` (such as conditions in `Piecewise`) from being rewritten or evaluated as sets.

### Solution
1. Replaced `Contains.as_set` with `_eval_as_set` in [`sympy/sets/contains.py`](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/sympy__sympy-23950/sympy/sets/contains.py) so that `Contains` inherits `Boolean.as_set(self)`:
   - For `Contains(x, S)` where `x` is a `Symbol` not free in `S`, `.as_set()` returns `S`.
   - For `Contains(expr, S)` where `expr` is not a symbol, it attempts to convert `S` to a relational via `S.as_relational(expr)` and call `.as_set()` on the resulting relational.
   - For univariate `Contains` expressions that cannot be simplified further, it falls back to `ConditionSet(x, self, S.Reals)`.
   - Multivariate expressions continue raising `NotImplementedError` via `Boolean.as_set()`.
2. Added test cases in [`sympy/sets/tests/test_contains.py`](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/sympy__sympy-23950/sympy/sets/tests/test_contains.py) verifying `.as_set()` behavior on `Contains`.
