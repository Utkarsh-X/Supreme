### Method Explanation
The problem asks for the rank of each person according to the rule:
- Initial rank starts at 1.
- Each unique score group receives a rank equal to $1 + (\text{number of people with a strictly higher score})$.

For each person $i$, their rank is calculated as $1 + \sum_{j: P_j > P_i} 1$. Since $N \le 100$, this $O(N^2)$ solution executes virtually instantaneously and satisfies all constraints.
