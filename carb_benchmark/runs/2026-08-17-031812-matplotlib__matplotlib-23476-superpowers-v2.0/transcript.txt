I have identified and resolved the issue where figure DPI doubled upon unpickling (particularly noticeable on macOS / HiDPI screens).

### Root Cause
When a `Figure` is created and associated with a HiDPI canvas (where `device_pixel_ratio` > 1, such as Retina screens), the figure's logical unscaled DPI is stored in `_original_dpi` (e.g. 100), while `_dpi` is scaled by the device pixel ratio (e.g. 200). 

When unpickled, `figure.__setstate__` re-instantiates `FigureCanvasBase(self)`. Previously, `FigureCanvasBase.__init__` unconditionally overwrote `figure._original_dpi` with `figure.dpi` (which was already 200). When the GUI canvas subsequently initialized and received a `_set_device_pixel_ratio(2)` event, it multiplied `_original_dpi` (now 200) by the device pixel ratio (2), doubling `fig.dpi` to 400. Repeated unpickling exponentially doubled the DPI.
