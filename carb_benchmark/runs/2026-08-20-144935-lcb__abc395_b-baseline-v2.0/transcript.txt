### Method Explanation

The problem asks us to simulate coloring an $N \times N$ grid. For $i = 1, 2, \dots, N$:
1. $j = N + 1 - i$.
2. If $i \le j$, we fill the subgrid with top-left corner $(i, i)$ and bottom-right corner $(j, j)$ (1-indexed) with `#` if $i$ is odd or `.` if $i$ is even. Overwriting any previous color.
3. If $i > j$, we stop.

Since $N \le 50$, directly simulating the step-by-step rectangular fill operations runs well within time limits ($O(N^3)$ operations at most).
