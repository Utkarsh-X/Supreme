### Method Explanation
1. Read input numbers \(N\), \(M\), and the sequence \(A = (A_1, A_2, \dots, A_M)\) from standard input.
2. Store elements of \(A\) in a hash set for \(O(1)\) lookup time.
3. Collect all integers \(x \in [1, N]\) that are not present in \(A\) in ascending order.
4. Output the count of missing numbers \(C\) on the first line, followed by the space-separated list of missing numbers on the second line.
