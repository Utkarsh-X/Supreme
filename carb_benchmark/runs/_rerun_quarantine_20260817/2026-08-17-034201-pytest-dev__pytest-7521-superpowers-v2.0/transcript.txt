### Root Cause & Fix

**Root Cause:**
In `src/_pytest/capture.py`, `FDCaptureBinary` initialized `self.tmpfile` using `EncodedFile` (which inherits from `io.TextIOWrapper`) without explicitly passing `newline=""`.
Because `newline` defaulted to `None`, Python's `TextIOWrapper` enabled universal newlines mode on input decoding, converting `\r` and `\r\n` characters to `\n` when `capfd.readouterr()` called `self.tmpfile.read()`. By contrast, `sys` capture (`capsys`) used `CaptureIO`, which explicitly specifies `newline=""` to preserve carriage return characters untranslated.

**Solution:**
1. Updated `FDCaptureBinary.__init__` in [`src/_pytest/capture.py`](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/pytest-dev__pytest-7521/src/_pytest/capture.py#L388-L392) to pass `newline=""` to `EncodedFile`.
2. Added `test_capturing_carriage_return` in [`testing/test_capture.py`](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/pytest-dev__pytest-7521/testing/test_capture.py#L1037-L1043) to verify that both `capfd` and `capsys` preserve `\r` and `\r\n` untranslated.
