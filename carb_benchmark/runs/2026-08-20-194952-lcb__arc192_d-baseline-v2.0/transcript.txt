### Method Explanation

1. **Prime Factorization Independence**:
   - For a sequence $S = (S_1, \dots, S_N)$ to satisfy $f(S_i / S_{i+1}) = A_i$ and $\gcd(S_1, \dots, S_N) = 1$, the prime factorization choices for each prime factor $p$ are completely independent.
   - For a prime factor $p$ and $a_{i, p} = v_p(A_i)$, at each step $i \in \{1, \dots, N-1\}$, we choose $d_{i, p} \in \{+a_{i, p}, -a_{i, p}\}$.
   - The sequence of prime exponents $e_{i, p} = v_p(S_i)$ is uniquely fixed up to an additive constant, and the condition $\gcd(S_1, \dots, S_N) = 1$ uniquely determines this constant by enforcing $\min_i e_{i, p} = 0$.

2. **Exponent Sum Reformulation**:
   - Let $E_{1, p} = 0$ and $E_{i+1, p} = E_{i, p} + d_{i, p}$. The total exponent sum for prime $p$ is $W_p = \sum_{i=1}^N E_{i, p} - N \min_k E_{k, p}$.
   - Defining $y_{j} = E_{j, p} - \min_{1 \le k \le j} E_{k, p}$, the recurrence relation for $y$ is $y_{j+1} = \max(0, y_j + d_j)$.
   - We can rewrite $W_p = \sum_{j=1}^{N-1} \Delta W_j$ where:
     - $\Delta W_j = y_{j+1} = y_j + a_j$ when $d_j = +a_j$.
     - $\Delta W_j = \max(0, y_j - a_j) + j \max(0, a_j - y_j)$ when $d_j = -a_j$.

3. **Dynamic Programming**:
   - For each prime factor $p$ present in $A$, we compute the sum of $p^{W_p} \pmod{998244353}$ using a dynamic programming state tracking the value $y$.
   - Consecutive zeros ($a_j = 0$) are grouped and processed in $O(1)$ per state.
   - Powers of $p$ are precomputed modulo $998244353$ to optimize transition times.
   - Finally, the total score sum over all good sequences is the product of the DP results for all relevant primes modulo $998244353$.
