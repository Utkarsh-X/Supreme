### Method Explanation
By analyzing the stack operations backwards from index $N-1$ down to $0$, we can model the relative stack height transitions.
Let $V_k$ be the maximum value obtained when considering the suffix of operations from index $N-1-k$ to $N-1$.
The DP recurrence simplifies to:
$$V_k = \max(V_{k-1} + A_{N-1-k}, V_{k-2})$$
with base values $V_{-1} = 0$ and $V_0 = A_{N-1}$.

This allows us to compute the maximum possible sum in $O(N)$ time and $O(1)$ space.
