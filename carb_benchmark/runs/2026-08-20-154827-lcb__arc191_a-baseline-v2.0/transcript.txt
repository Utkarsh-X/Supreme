### Summary of Approach
1. **Mathematical Structure**:
   - Any sequence of $M$ replacement operations allows choosing $v$ optional digits from $T[0 \dots M-2]$ ($0 \le v \le \min(N-1, M-1)$) alongside the mandatory final digit $T[M-1]$.
   - The selected $v$ optional digits can be chosen as the top $v$ largest digits of $T[0 \dots M-2]$.
   - The combined multiset $D_v = P[0 \dots v-1] \cup \{T[M-1]\}$ (sorted descending) is then placed into $v+1$ positions in $S$ to maximize $S'$ lexicographically.

2. **Linear Time Evaluation ($O(N)$)**:
   - For a fixed multiset $D_v$, the optimal placement in $S$ is computed greedily from left to right.
   - When $D[idx] > S[j]$, overwriting is strictly optimal.
   - When $D[idx] < S[j]$, keeping $S[j]$ is strictly optimal.
   - When $D[idx] == S[j]$, we look at $\min(S[j+1 \dots N-1])$. If there exists a smaller digit later, keeping $S[j]$ allows $D[idx]$ to improve a future position; otherwise, overwriting consumes $D[idx]$ immediately without loss.
   - Early loop breaks occur as soon as $D$ is exhausted or all remaining positions must be overwritten.

3. **Unimodal Optimization & Ternary Search**:
   - The maximum string $S'_v$ as a function of $v$ is strictly unimodal.
   - Ternary search over $v \in [0, \min(N-1, M-1)]$ finds the optimal $v^*$ in $O(\log (\min(N, M)))$ steps, running in $\approx 0.6$ seconds for $N, M = 10^6$.
