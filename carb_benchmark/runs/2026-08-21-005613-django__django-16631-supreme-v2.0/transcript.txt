### Cause of the Issue
`AbstractBaseUser.get_session_auth_hash()` hardcoded calls to `salted_hmac` without passing a custom `secret` parameter (defaulting strictly to `settings.SECRET_KEY`). Additionally, `django.contrib.auth.get_user()` and `login()` only verified session authentication hashes against `user.get_session_auth_hash()`. When `SECRET_KEY` was rotated and the old key moved to `SECRET_KEY_FALLBACKS`, existing logged-in session hashes (computed with the previous key) failed validation and triggered session flushing.
