### Method Explanation

We want to maximize $D(1, i) + D(i+1, j) + D(j+1, N)$ over $1 \le i < j \le N-1$, where $D(l, r)$ is the number of distinct elements in $A[l \dots r]$.

1. **Precomputations**:
   - $L[i] = D(1, i)$: distinct count in prefix $A[1 \dots i]$, precomputed in $O(N)$ time.
   - $R[j] = D(j+1, N)$: distinct count in suffix $A[j+1 \dots N]$, precomputed in $O(N)$ time.

2. **Dynamic Range Increments**:
   - For a fixed right boundary $j$, as $j$ advances from $1$ to $N-1$, adding $A[j]$ causes element $A[j]$ to appear in subarray $A[i+1 \dots j]$ for all $i \in [\max(1, \text{prev}[A[j]]), j-1]$, where $\text{prev}[x]$ is the previous 1-based index of element value $x$.
   - Thus, moving from $j-1$ to $j$ increases $D(i+1, j)$ by $+1$ for all active indices $i \in [\max(1, \text{prev}[A[j]]), j-1]$.

3. **Segment Tree & Complexity**:
   - We use a non-recursive, zero-push iterative segment tree over $i \in [1, N-1]$ to maintain $L[i] + D(i+1, j)$.
   - At step $j$, index $j-1$ is activated with value $L[j-1] + 1$, range additions of $+1$ are applied to range $[\max(1, \text{prev}[A[j]]), j-2]$, and global maximum `tree[1]` $+ R[j]$ is checked in $O(1)$ time.
   - Overall Time Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(N \log N)$
   - Overall Space Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(N)$
