### Method Summary
1. **Mathematical Observation**:
   - Each operation allows moving all stones from bag $A$ into bag $B$. 
   - Repeating this operation partitions the set of initial $N$ bags into disjoint non-empty groups $G_1, G_2, \dots, G_k$.
   - Every element in a group $G_m$ contributes its stones to the same final bag, yielding a sum of $S(G_m) = \sum_{i \in G_m} A_i$.
   - Any set partition of $\{1, 2, \dots, N\}$ can be achieved through a sequence of operations.
   - The resulting XOR sum of final bag values is $S(G_1) \oplus S(G_2) \oplus \dots \oplus S(G_k)$.
