### Method Explanation
1. **Combinatorial Frequency**: For a grid of size $m \times n$ with total cells $N = m \cdot n$, any chosen pair of cells $c_1, c_2$ contains a pair of pieces in $\binom{N-2}{k-2}$ valid arrangements of $k$ pieces.
2. **Sum of Pairwise Distances**: The sum of Manhattan distances over all cell pairs on an $m \times n$ grid decomposes independently into row differences and column differences:
   $$D = \sum_{c_1 \neq c_2} (|x_1 - x_2| + |y_1 - y_2|) = n^2 \binom{m+1}{3} + m^2 \binom{n+1}{3}$$
3. **Total Sum**: The total sum across all valid arrangements is $\binom{N-2}{k-2} \times D \pmod{10^9 + 7}$.

### Complexity
- **Time Complexity**: $\mathcal{O}(m \cdot n)$ to precompute factorials for combination counts.
- **Space Complexity**: $\mathcal{O}(m \cdot n)$ space for the factorial arrays.
