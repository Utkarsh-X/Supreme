### Solution Summary
- **Approach**: Breadth-First Search (BFS) over the state space of pair of vertices $(u, v)$, representing paths from $u$ to $v$.
  - **Base Cases**: 
    - Length 0 palindromes: All pairs $(i, i)$ with distance `0` (empty string).
    - Length 1 palindromes: All pairs $(i, j)$ where a directed edge $i \to j$ exists with distance `1` (single character string).
  - **Transitions**: When popping state $(u', v')$ with distance $d$, we transition to state $(u, v)$ with distance $d + 2$ for all edges $u \to u'$ labeled $c$ and $v' \to v$ labeled $c$.
- **Optimization**: Bitwise operations (bitmasks) are used to keep track of unvisited target vertices for each source vertex $u$. This reduces the search step from $O(N^4)$ down to $O(N^3)$, taking $< 0.1$ seconds even for dense graphs with $N=100$.
