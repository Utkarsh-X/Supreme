### Method Explanation
1. Let $d = x - y$. Since $x > y \ge 1$, $d$ must be a positive integer divisor of $N$.
2. Substituting $x = y + d$ into $x^3 - y^3 = N$ gives:
   $$N = d(3y^2 + 3dy + d^2)$$
3. Since $y \ge 1$ and $d \ge 1$, we have $3y^2 + 3dy > 0$, which implies $N > d^3$ or $d < N^{1/3} \le 10^6$.
4. We iterate through all candidate integer values of $d$ from $1$ up to $\lfloor (N-1)^{1/3} \rfloor$.
5. For each $d$ dividing $N$, let $k = N / d$. We solve the quadratic equation $3y^2 + 3dy + (d^2 - k) = 0$ for $y$:
   $$\Delta = 3(4k - d^2), \quad y = \frac{-3d + \sqrt{\Delta}}{6}$$
6. If $\Delta$ is a perfect square and $y$ is a positive integer, then $(x, y) = (y + d, y)$ is a valid solution.
7. If no such $d$ yields a valid $(x, y)$, output `-1`.
