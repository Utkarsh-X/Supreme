### Solution Explanation

1. **Mathematical Property**:
   - A positive integer $N$ is a "400 number" if and only if it has exactly 2 distinct prime factors and all prime factor exponents in its prime factorization are positive even integers.
   - Any number whose prime factor exponents are all even is a perfect square $N = X^2$.
   - The set of prime factors of $N = X^2$ is identical to the set of prime factors of $X$.
   - Therefore, $N$ is a 400 number if and only if $N = X^2$ where $X$ is a positive integer with **exactly 2 distinct prime factors** ($\omega(X) = 2$).

2. **Precomputation**:
   - Since $A \le 10^{12}$, $X \le \sqrt{10^{12}} = 10^6$.

   - We store $X^2$ for all $X$ where $\omega(X) = 2$ in a sorted array `valid_N`.

3. **Query Processing**:
   - For each query $A$, we use `bisect.bisect_right` on `valid_N` to quickly locate the largest 400 number not exceeding $A$ in $O(\log K)$ time.
