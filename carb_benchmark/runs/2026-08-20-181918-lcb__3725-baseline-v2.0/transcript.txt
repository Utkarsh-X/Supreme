### Summary of Approach
1. **Duality of Min and Max**:
   The total sum of $(\min + \max)$ over all valid subarrays equals the sum of maximums of all subarrays of `nums` minus the sum of maximums of all subarrays of `-nums`. Thus, the problem reduces to calculating the sum of maximum elements of all subarrays of length at most $k$.

2. **Monotonic Stack & Boundaries**:
   For each element $nums[i]$, we find:
   - $L_i$: nearest index to the left with $nums[L_i] > nums[i]$ (strictly greater)
   - $R_i$: nearest index to the right with $nums[R_i] \ge nums[i]$ (greater than or equal)

   $nums[i]$ is the maximum element for any subarray starting at index $l \in [L_i + 1, i]$ and ending at index $r \in [i, R_i - 1]$.

3. **Inclusion-Exclusion Counting under Length Restriction**:
   Let $a = i - L_i - 1$ and $b = R_i - i - 1$. A subarray $nums[l..r]$ has length at most $k$ if $(i - l) + (r - i) \le k - 1$.
   Setting $x = i - l$ and $y = r - i$, we count non-negative integer pairs $(x, y)$ such that $0 \le x \le a$, $0 \le y \le b$, and $x + y \le M$ (where $M = k - 1$).
   Using 2D inclusion-exclusion, the exact count of valid pairs is:
   \[
   f(a, b, M) = T(M) - T(M - a - 1) - T(M - b - 1) + T(M - a - b - 2)
   \]
   where $T(S) = \frac{(S+1)(S+2)}{2}$ for $S \ge 0$ (and $0$ for $S < 0$).

4. **Time & Space Complexity**:
   - **Time Complexity**: $\mathcal{O}(N)$ since finding $L_i$ and $R_i$ using monotonic stack takes $\mathcal{O}(N)$, and computing counts for all elements takes $\mathcal{O}(N)$.
   - **Space Complexity**: $\mathcal{O}(N)$ for array allocations.
