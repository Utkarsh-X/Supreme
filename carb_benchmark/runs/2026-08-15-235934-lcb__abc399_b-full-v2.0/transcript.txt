### Method Explanation
The problem requires assigning ranks based on scores, where the rank of each person corresponds to the starting rank index $r$ when processing scores in non-increasing order. 

Equivalently, the rank of a person with score $P_i$ is equal to:
$$\text{Rank}(P_i) = 1 + |\{j : P_j > P_i\}|$$

That is, $1$ plus the number of people who scored strictly higher than person $i$.
