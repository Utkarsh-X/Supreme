I have fixed the issue where `is_even` (and other number set assumptions like `is_odd`, `is_integer`, `is_rational`, and `is_algebraic`) did not imply `is_finite`.

### Root Cause
In SymPy's assumptions system (`sympy/core/assumptions.py` and `sympy/assumptions/ask.py`), algebraic numbers were established as complex numbers (`algebraic -> complex`), but there was no rule stating that algebraic numbers (and by extension rational numbers, integers, even/odd integers, primes, etc.) are finite (`algebraic -> finite`). Consequently, declaring a symbol with `even=True` or `integer=True` did not deduce `is_finite = True`.

### Fix
1. Added `algebraic -> finite` rule to `_assume_rules` in [sympy/core/assumptions.py](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/sympy__sympy-16597/sympy/core/assumptions.py#L169). By deduction through `rational -> algebraic` and `integer -> rational`, symbols with `algebraic`, `rational`, `integer`, `even`, `odd`, `prime`, or `composite` assumptions now automatically deduce `is_finite = True`.
2. Updated definitions for `transcendental`, `irrational`, and `noninteger` in `_assume_rules` to require `finite`, ensuring infinite quantities (`oo`, `-oo`) correctly evaluate to `False` for these properties instead of `None` or `True`.
3. Updated `get_known_facts()` in [sympy/assumptions/ask.py](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/sympy__sympy-16597/sympy/assumptions/ask.py#L1488) to include `Implies(Q.algebraic, Q.finite)` and updated [sympy/assumptions/ask_generated.py](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/sympy__sympy-16597/sympy/assumptions/ask_generated.py) via `bin/ask_update.py`.
4. Added test `test_even_is_finite()` in [sympy/core/tests/test_assumptions.py](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/sympy__sympy-16597/sympy/core/tests/test_assumptions.py#L1066) and updated test assertions for infinite quantities.
