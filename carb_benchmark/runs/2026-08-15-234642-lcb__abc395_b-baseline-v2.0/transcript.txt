### Summary of Implementation
- **Simulation**: We maintain an $N \times N$ grid. For each step $i = 1, 2, \dots, N$:
  - Calculate $j = N + 1 - i$.
  - If $i \le j$, we update the subgrid with top-left corner $(i, i)$ and bottom-right corner $(j, j)$ to `#` if $i$ is odd, and `.` if $i$ is even (1-indexed).
- **Output**: After processing all operations, the grid is printed row by row to standard output.
