### Summary of Changes

#### Issue Identified
`floatformat('0.00', 0)` and `floatformat(Decimal('0.00'), 0)` threw `ValueError: valid range for prec is [1, MAX_PREC]`. 

This occurred because when `d` had no non-zero fractional part (`m = int(d) - d` evaluated to `0` or `Decimal('0.00')`), `units` was calculated as `len(tupl[1]) + tupl[2]`. For values like `'0.00'`, `tupl[1]` is `(0,)` (length 1) and `tupl[2]` is `-2`, resulting in `units = -1`. When `p = 0`, `prec = abs(p) + units + 1` evaluated to `0`, causing Python's `decimal.Context(prec=prec)` to raise a `ValueError`.

#### Fix
- In [`django/template/defaultfilters.py`](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/django__django-16485/django/template/defaultfilters.py#L183-L187), clamped `units = max(1, units)` so that `units` is always at least `1` (representing at least 1 integer digit `"0"`), ensuring `prec` is always `>= 2`.
- In [`tests/template_tests/filter_tests/test_floatformat.py`](file:///E:/RofU/Supreme/carb_workspaces/django__django-16485/tests/template_tests/filter_tests/test_floatformat.py#L105-L110), added tests for `floatformat("0.00", 0)` and `floatformat(Decimal("0.00"), 0)`.
