I have implemented the solution for finding the sum of all elements in the 9-by-9 multiplication table that are not equal to $X$.

### Implementation Details
- The total sum of all values in the 9-by-9 multiplication table is generated by iterating $i$ from 1 to 9 and $j$ from 1 to 9.
- For each cell $(i, j)$, if $i \times j \neq X$, its value is added to the running total.
- The solution reads $X$ from standard input and prints the computed sum to standard output.
