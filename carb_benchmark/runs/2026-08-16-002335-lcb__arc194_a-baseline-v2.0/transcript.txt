### Method Explanation
1. **Operation Structure Analysis**:
   - Each operation at index $i \in [1, N]$ is either a `Push` (appends $A_i$ to $S$) or a `Pop` (deletes top element of $S$).
   - Any `Pop` at step $j$ pops the top of stack, which was pushed at some $i < j$. Because a stack is LIFO, matched push-pop pairs form valid balanced parenthesis subsegments of even length $2m \ge 2$, contributing a net sum of 0 to $S$.
   - Any `Push` not popped by a later `Pop` remains in $S$ at the end and contributes $+A_k$ to the final sum.
   - Therefore, the problem reduces to partitioning $A[1 \dots N]$ into:
     - Single elements $A_k$ (which stay in $S$, contributing $A_k$).
     - Contiguous even-length blocks (which are popped, contributing 0).

2. **Dynamic Programming**:
   - Let $dp[i]$ be the maximum possible sum on prefix $A[1 \dots i]$.
   - For step $i$:
     - **Option 1** (Single element): $dp[i] = dp[i-1] + A_i$.
     - **Option 2** (End of even-length block): $dp[i] = \max_{k \le i-2, (i-k) \text{ is even}} dp[k]$.
   - By maintaining running maximums `max_even` and `max_odd` for even/odd prefixes, each step $i$ is computed in $O(1)$ time and $O(N)$ space.
