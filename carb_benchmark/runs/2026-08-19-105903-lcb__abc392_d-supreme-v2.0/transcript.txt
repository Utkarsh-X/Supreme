### Solution Explanation
1. **Probability Calculation**:
   For die $i$ with $K_i$ faces, we calculate the probability of rolling each value $x$ as $P(i, x) = \frac{\text{count}(x)}{K_i}$.
   When choosing two distinct dice $i$ and $j$, the probability that both dice show the same face value is:
   $$P(i, j) = \sum_{x} P(i, x) \cdot P(j, x)$$
2. **Optimization**:
   Since $N \le 100$, we iterate through all distinct pairs of dice $(i, j)$ with $1 \le i < j \le N$. For each pair, we iterate over the probability distribution of the die with fewer unique face values to quickly calculate the dot product.
